**Alternates**

**What would make this wrong**
- Waxed cotton needs rewaxing every ~2 seasons and transfers wax onto light
  fabrics for the first month. If that's annoying, the pick is wrong — not
  defective, just not for you. *(Likelihood: moderate. This is the real risk.)*
- Northmoor's returns are 14 days from **delivery**, not order. Tight but passing.

- Bellweather Chore — €89, but 3-star reviews cluster on seams failing at 6 months.

**One thing away**
- **Volebrook Field Jacket** would qualify if the ceiling moved €35. Materially
  better shell. Worth knowing before you decide the ceiling is final.

**Timing:** Buy now. Waxed outerwear bottoms out in **February**, not autumn —
waiting costs you the season and saves maybe 15%.
