# Skeptic pass — the case against

## Target 1: Corso Nylon Shell (original top scorer, €175)

**Killed.** Scored highest on value and passed every gate, but:

- 3-star reviews: **11 of 14** mention the shell "sounding loud" and reading
  sportier in person than in photos. Brief says smart-casual over knitwear.
- Seller is a marketplace reseller, registered 7 months. No brand-direct option.
- "Water resistant" is an untreated DWR coating — gone in roughly 20 washes,
  and unlike wax it can't be renewed.

→ Demoted. Runner-up promoted and re-attacked.

## Target 2: Northmoor Waxed Chore (promoted, €210)

**Survived, with two objections that made it into the dossier.**

- ✅ Brand-direct, 11 years trading, physical returns address in the EU.
- ✅ Sizing: 9 T3 reports, consistent — true to size with a layer. Unusually clean.
- ⚠️ **Wax transfer.** First-month wax transfer onto light-colored fabrics is
  well documented. Not a defect, but a real lifestyle cost and the principal
  hasn't owned waxed cotton before. → dossier.
- ⚠️ **Returns clock starts at delivery**, not order. 14 days is the profile's
  hard floor — this passes by zero margin. → dossier.
- ❌ Checked: is €210 real? 90-day median €215, floor €189 (Black Friday). Not a
  fake discount, not a special low either. Fairly priced, and that's the honest
  framing rather than calling it a deal.

**Verdict: pick stands.** Both objections disclosed rather than suppressed.
